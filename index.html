I develop hierarchical AI frameworks that integrate deep and multi-agent reinforcement learning with model predictive control and convex optimization — enabling connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) to coordinate safely and efficiently in mixed-traffic highway environments. My work spans algorithm design, SUMO/CARLA simulation, and collaboration with ORNL toward real-world validation of autonomous mobility systems.

Published result (Control Engineering Practice, Elsevier 2025): Hierarchical DRL + optimal control framework for CAV on-ramp merging: +12.5% mainline throughput, −28% emergency braking events, −31.66% fuel consumption versus baseline.

I hold an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from UTK (2025) and an M.S. in Soft Computing from Universiti Malaysia Pahang (2022), where I earned the 1st Place Excellent Publication Award and a Gold Medal at Malaysia Technology Expo. With 886 Google Scholar citations (h-index 14) and a 4.9/5.0 teaching effectiveness rating, I am actively seeking tenure-track faculty and research scientist positions beginning Spring 2027.

What I work on

Research Interests

🏗️
Hierarchical RL & Temporal Abstraction
Options framework and hierarchical DRL architectures for multi-level vehicle decision-making; improved sample efficiency and interpretability over flat RL.
🚗
CAV On-Ramp Merging
Solving highway on-ramp merging — a primary congestion and fuel-waste source — through three simulation phases: single-ramp, mixed autonomy, and corridor-wide control.
🤝
Multi-Agent RL & Cooperative Control
MARL controllers for CAV platoons to coordinate across successive highway sections under mixed autonomy, toward ORNL real-world validation.
⚙️
Optimal Control & MPC
Integrating model predictive control and pseudospectral convex optimization with learning-based methods to guarantee safety-constrained, dynamically feasible trajectories.
👤
Human–Autonomy Interaction
Human–vehicle interaction and AI teaming for automated driving and advanced air mobility (NSF CAREER RISE-AAM, Award No. 2237215).
⚡
Energy-Efficient ITS
U.S. DOE-funded collaboration with ORNL on the CAV–energy nexus; hierarchical intelligent control achieving 31.66% fleet-level fuel reduction vs. baseline.
